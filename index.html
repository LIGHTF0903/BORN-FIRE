About Calling Choir

Calling Choir (콜링콰이어) is Korea's first hip-hop gospel show choir, blending dynamic vocal harmony with urban street dance, B-boys and a live band. Based in Seoul, the choir carries one message onto every stage: truth into the world's culture.

BORN FIRE began as a live gospel show made for the next generation outside the church. Fans backed it through crowdfunding, and on March 7, 2026 the whole night was captured live. Built around the theme of “God's spark of fire”, the album brings ten original songs and two bonus stages to the world.

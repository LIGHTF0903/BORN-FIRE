The Album

Born Fire

Recorded live at 'Born Fire: Calling Live Show Vol.1' in Seoul on March 7, 2026, this album captures the whole night exactly as it happened.

Most of the songs were written by the members themselves, layering hip-hop, a live band and street dance over Black gospel. It's an attempt to bring gospel out of the church and into the culture, where people actually are.
